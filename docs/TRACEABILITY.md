## 5. How to keep this matrix honest

1. Every commit references its TODO item `[N]`; every closed item updates the
   row(s) above with the real test path.
2. Phase 10 (item 91) froze this matrix and keeps the generated companion
   (`docs/reports/traceability.md`) current: `scripts/traceability_report.py`
   maps each requirement to modules and tests, asserts both exist and that
   the tests collect, and is itself covered by
   `tests/unit/test_traceability_report.py`.
3. A requirement may not be marked covered by a test that does not exist in
   `tests/` — planned rows are explicitly labelled, and the pending usability
   area is labelled `PENDING` rather than folded into the proven rows.
